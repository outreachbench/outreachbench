Expandi Alternatives in 2026: 7 LinkedIn Tools Ranked by Account Safety

MR Marcus R. · outreachbench_ · Updated May 2026

If you are searching for Expandi alternatives, the reason is probably not that Expandi is a bad product. It is not. Expandi is one of the older cloud-based LinkedIn automation platforms, it has real users, and it ships a real feature set. The reason people leave is more specific. They hit a wall — a feature wall, a price wall, or an account-safety wall — and the wall is structural, not fixable by tweaking settings inside Expandi.

This article ranks seven Expandi competitors against the same three walls. Safety, price, and fit. The short version: BriskReach is the only tool in this category that lets you opt out of automation entirely through its Reps model, and its BYO mode is the most conservative automation engine on the market at $39 per seat — undercutting Expandi's entry tier by roughly $10.

What is Expandi, and what does it actually do?

Expandi is a cloud-based LinkedIn automation tool launched in 2019 and built around three pillars: dedicated IP addresses per account, smart sequences that adapt to prospect behavior, and integrations with CRMs like HubSpot and Salesforce. It runs on Expandi's servers, not as a browser extension, which was a meaningful safety upgrade when the platform launched because it removed the fingerprinting risk that extension-based tools like Octopus CRM and Dux-Soup carried.

The product is legitimate. G2 reviews on Expandi cluster around 4.4 out of 5, and the company has been in the LinkedIn automation space long enough that the platform has real feature depth. Webhooks, A/B testing on message variants, image and GIF personalization, dynamic placeholders, and a campaign builder that supports conditional logic. The team behind it ships updates regularly and maintains a public roadmap.

But Expandi is not a tool you scale into without friction. The walls show up at three specific points, and every Expandi alternative in this list is built to knock down at least one of them.

Why are people searching for Expandi alternatives in 2026?

The Expandi alternatives search has grown for a reason. Here are the four most common ones we see in 2026.

Price scaling is brutal. Expandi charges $99 per account per month on the upper tier. An agency running 10 LinkedIn profiles pays roughly $990 per month on Expandi alone, before any CRM or data-enrichment costs are added. There is no unlimited-accounts plan.

Cluster detection at scale. Expandi's dedicated IP per account works well for a single profile. It works less well when 20 accounts connected to the same dashboard all send connection requests to overlapping prospect lists within the same time window. LinkedIn's 2026 detection systems track account clusters, IP relationships, and connection-graph similarities across the network. Users running Expandi at agency scale report synchronized restrictions across multiple accounts after a single detection event.

The platform has not kept pace with AI. Expandi's message builder is template-based. There is no native AI-assisted reply handling, no intent-based lead prioritization, and no multichannel orchestration. Newer tools like BriskReach and Salesflow ship these features out of the box.

The interface feels dated. Multiple 2026 reviews flag a UI that has not been redesigned since 2022. Campaign setup is functional, not modern, and the analytics layer is shallow compared to HeyReach or Dripify.

None of these weaknesses make Expandi a bad product. They make it the wrong product for a specific set of users. If you are running one LinkedIn account, sending fewer than 200 connection requests a month, and you do not need AI or multichannel — Expandi is fine. If you fall outside that profile, here are the seven tools that solve what Expandi does not.

2. HeyReach — Best for agencies running 5+ accounts simultaneously

Best for: Agencies that manage many LinkedIn profiles from a single dashboard
Pricing: $79 per single sender, $199 for unlimited senders
Website: heyreach.io

HeyReach was built specifically for agency use. Its primary differentiator is multi-account management — you can connect 50 or more LinkedIn profiles to a single dashboard and distribute outreach across all of them. The platform uses account rotation so that no single profile sends at high volume, which theoretically reduces per-account detection risk. A unified inbox centralizes replies from every connected account, and white-label reporting makes the tool suitable for client-facing agency work.

Where HeyReach falls short against Expandi: The $199 unlimited plan encourages high-volume usage across many accounts. LinkedIn's 2026 detection tracks IP clusters, device fingerprint patterns, and connection-graph similarity. When ten accounts on the same dashboard all send to the same target demographic within the same time window, LinkedIn detects the coordination. Agencies report losing multiple client accounts at once after a single detection event.

Where HeyReach beats Expandi: Multi-account rotation, a unified inbox, agency-specific client reporting, and faster campaign deployment across many profiles. If you run an agency and accept the cluster-detection trade-off, HeyReach is more capable than Expandi for that use case.

3. Dripify — Best for users who want a simpler, cloud-based Expandi alternative

Best for: Solo operators and small teams who want Expandi's cloud-based safety without the enterprise price tag
Pricing: $59 to $99 per LinkedIn account per month (annual billing saves roughly $20 per tier)
Website: dripify.io

Dripify is the closest direct competitor to Expandi on the list. It is cloud-based, runs dedicated infrastructure per account, and ships a campaign builder that is simpler than Expandi's without losing the core features. Multi-step drips, A/B testing, a centralized inbox, basic analytics, and integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho CRM.

The tool is legitimate. Trustpilot reviews on Dripify cluster around 4.2 out of 5, the user base is large, and the product is stable.

Where Dripify falls short against Expandi: Less feature depth. Expandi's smart sequences, conditional logic, and webhook infrastructure are more mature than Dripify's. Agencies managing complex multi-touch campaigns may find Dripify limiting.

Where Dripify beats Expandi: Pricing on the lower tiers ($59 vs $99), a cleaner interface, and a faster learning curve. If you do not need Expandi's enterprise-grade features, Dripify is a meaningful step up on usability. (For a deeper comparison, see Dripify Competitors & Alternatives in 2026: 7 Tools Ranked by Account Safety.)

4. Zopto — Best for enterprise sales teams with deep CRM needs

Best for: Enterprise sales teams running 50+ LinkedIn accounts with deep Salesforce or HubSpot integration requirements
Pricing: $197 to $497 per month (custom pricing above 50 accounts)
Website: zopto.com

Zopto is the most enterprise-positioned tool on this list. The platform ships with native Salesforce and HubSpot integrations, advanced filtering on LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ABM (account-based marketing) workflows, and a reporting layer built for sales operations teams. The UI is the most polished of any LinkedIn automation tool we have tested.

Where Zopto falls short against Expandi: Price. Zopto's $197 starting tier is roughly double Expandi's $99. The platform is also heavily US/EU focused, with limited support for APAC timezones and prospect lists.

Where Zopto beats Expandi: CRM integration depth, reporting, and a UI that does not feel like a 2019 LinkedIn tool. If your sales ops team lives in Salesforce and your budget is not the constraint, Zopto is more capable than Expandi at enterprise scale.

5. Octopus CRM — Best for users who prefer browser-extension workflows

Best for: Users who want LinkedIn automation directly in the browser without a separate cloud dashboard
Pricing: $9.99 to $74.99 per month depending on tier
Website: octopuscrm.io

Octopus CRM is a Chrome extension, not a cloud-based platform. It lives in your browser and runs actions from a popup UI overlaid on LinkedIn. The price is the lowest on this list — the basic tier starts at $9.99 per month, which makes it the cheapest Expandi alternative by a wide margin.

Where Octopus CRM falls short against Expandi: Extension-based tools carry the highest detection risk of any LinkedIn automation category. LinkedIn's 2026 detection systems can identify Chrome extensions, browser fingerprint patterns, and locally-initiated actions much more reliably than server-side behavior. Users running Octopus CRM at scale report restriction rates significantly higher than cloud-based competitors. Expandi's cloud-based architecture is structurally safer than Octopus's extension model. (For a deeper analysis of extension-based detection risk, see LinkedIn Account Restricted by Automation: What's Actually Happening.)

Where Octopus CRM beats Expandi: Price, simplicity, and zero server setup. If you are running a single LinkedIn account at low volume and price is the only constraint, Octopus CRM is the cheapest functional option.

6. Salesflow — Best for users who want AI-assisted replies and modern UI

Best for: Teams that want AI-assisted message drafting and reply handling on top of a cloud-based automation engine
Pricing: $59 to $99 per account per month
Website: salesflow.io

Salesflow is one of the newer cloud-based LinkedIn automation tools, and the platform has invested heavily in AI features. The message builder includes AI-assisted drafting, the reply handler uses intent detection to flag high-priority responses, and the warmup sequence is more aggressive than Expandi's. The UI is modern, fast, and built for daily-use comfort.

Where Salesflow falls short against Expandi: The platform is younger, and the integration ecosystem is shallower. Zoho, HubSpot, and Salesforce are supported, but webhook and custom-event coverage is limited compared to Expandi's mature API.

Where Salesflow beats Expandi: AI-assisted replies, modern UI, and faster campaign setup. If AI is the deciding feature and Expandi's dated interface is what drove you to search for alternatives, Salesflow is the closest functional replacement on the list.

7. LinkedHelper — Best for users who want desktop-app control

Best for: Power users who prefer a desktop application over a cloud dashboard
Pricing: $15 to $99 per month depending on tier
Website: linkedhelper.com

LinkedHelper is a desktop application that runs on your machine, not in the cloud. The tool is one of the oldest in the category and has a deep feature set, including a built-in CRM, auto-visit, auto-endorse, and advanced filtering on Sales Navigator. The price on the lower tiers is competitive.

Where LinkedHelper falls short against Expandi: Desktop-based tools run on your machine's IP and device fingerprint, which LinkedIn can track. The platform's safety record is weaker than Expandi's cloud-based infrastructure, and the UI is the most dated on this list. LinkedHelper has not shipped a major redesign since 2021.

Where LinkedHelper beats Expandi: Price on the lower tiers, a built-in CRM, and the deepest feature set of any desktop LinkedIn tool. If you prefer local control over cloud-based automation, LinkedHelper is the most functional option.

Frequently asked questions

Is Expandi worth it in 2026?
Expandi is worth it for users running a single LinkedIn account at fewer than 200 connection requests per month with no AI or multichannel requirements. It is not worth it for users who need to scale beyond a single account, want AI-assisted features, or want to eliminate the automation risk entirely through a Reps model like BriskReach offers.

What is the cheapest Expandi alternative?
The cheapest functional Expandi alternative is Octopus CRM at $9.99 per month on the entry tier. The cheapest cloud-based alternative is BriskReach BYO at $39 per seat, which is also the most conservative safety engine in the category. LinkedHelper's $15 tier is the cheapest desktop-based option.

Is there a tool that eliminates LinkedIn account risk entirely?
BriskReach Reps mode eliminates the automation target by using rented LinkedIn accounts to send connection requests and messages through the platform. LinkedIn's detection systems are built to identify automation behavior, and outreach through established rented accounts operates outside the scope of typical automation detection. The trade-off is cost ($129 per Rep per month) and a 14-day ramp before the Rep is live.

Which Expandi alternative is best for agencies?
HeyReach is the most agency-focused Expandi alternative on the market. It ships multi-account management for 50+ profiles, a unified inbox, white-label reporting, and account rotation. The trade-off is cluster-detection risk when many accounts target the same prospect list within the same time window.

Can I migrate my Expandi campaigns to another tool?
Campaign migration between LinkedIn automation tools is manual in every case. There is no universal export format. Most Expandi alternatives (BriskReach, Dripify, Salesflow) will rebuild your campaigns from screenshots in 30 to 60 minutes per campaign.

Does Expandi have a free trial?
Expandi does not offer a free trial in 2026. The platform offers a 7-day money-back guarantee on annual plans only. BriskReach, Dripify, and Salesflow all offer free trials ranging from 7 to 14 days.

What is the difference between Expandi and BriskReach?
Expandi is a cloud-based LinkedIn automation tool that runs campaigns from your account through dedicated IPs. BriskReach is a platform that ships two modes — BYO automation through a more conservative safety engine, and a Reps model that provides rented LinkedIn accounts for running outreach through the platform. The structural difference is that Expandi manages the risk of automation, while BriskReach Reps eliminates the target entirely.
