HeyReach Review 2026: Features, Pricing, Account Safety, and a Categorical Alternative

HeyReach is the most agency-focused LinkedIn automation tool in 2026, and the platform has become the default pick for agencies that need to manage many LinkedIn accounts from a single dashboard. The product launched in 2021 and was built around a specific use case: multi-account rotation, a unified inbox, and white-label reporting for client-facing agency work.

This review covers what HeyReach actually does, what it costs, how safe it is for your LinkedIn accounts in 2026, and where it falls short. What is HeyReach, and who is it built for?

HeyReach is a cloud-based LinkedIn automation platform launched in 2021 by a team based in Finland. The product was designed around a single use case — managing many LinkedIn accounts from a single dashboard — and the feature set reflects that positioning. The platform is not built for solo operators with one LinkedIn account. It is built for agencies that need to run 5, 10, or 50+ profiles for client work.

The platform's primary differentiator is multi-account rotation. HeyReach distributes outreach volume across all connected accounts so that no single profile sends at high volume in a given time window. The unified inbox centralizes replies from every connected account. White-label reporting makes the tool suitable for client-facing agency deliverables. The UI is modern, fast, and built for daily-use comfort across many profiles.

HeyReach is the most agency-focused tool in the LinkedIn automation category, and the platform's G2 reviews reflect that — reviews cluster around 4.5 out of 5, with the highest marks going to multi-account management, the unified inbox, and the white-label reports. What features does HeyReach ship in 2026?

HeyReach's feature set is built around the multi-account agency use case, and the platform has added AI features and integration depth over the last 12 months.

Multi-account rotation. The platform's flagship feature. Connect 5 to 50+ LinkedIn accounts to a single dashboard and distribute outreach volume across all of them. Per-profile daily caps, warmup schedules, and safety controls let agencies tune the volume each connected account sends. The rotation logic runs at the campaign level, not the workspace level, which means different campaigns can target different accounts.

Unified inbox. Replies from every connected account land in a single inbox, with per-account filtering and per-campaign tagging. This is the most meaningful productivity feature for agencies running 5+ client accounts, and it is the feature that drives most of the "HeyReach vs Dripify" comparisons in this category.

White-label reports. The platform ships report templates that agencies can brand with their own logo, color palette, and client name. The reports cover campaign performance, reply rates, connection acceptance rates, and per-account activity. This is the feature that makes HeyReach the default pick for client-facing agency work.

Reply detection and intent flagging. HeyReach uses basic intent detection on replies to flag high-priority responses. The feature is not as mature as Salesflow's AI-assisted reply handling, but it is a meaningful upgrade over template-based tools like Dripify or Expandi.

CRM integrations. Native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho. Webhooks for custom integrations. The integration ecosystem is not as deep as Expandi's, but it covers the most common agency use cases.

A/B testing on message variants. A/B testing on connection request messages and follow-up sequences. The platform supports up to 8 variants per campaign, with statistical significance reporting on the back end.

Per-profile safety controls. Timezone targeting, working hours, daily caps, and warmup curves are configurable per profile. This is more granular than Expandi's per-profile settings and meaningfully better than Octopus CRM's global settings.

The feature set is mature. HeyReach has been in the category for 5 years, and the platform has shipped consistent updates over that period. The product is stable, the support team is responsive, and the public roadmap is active.

How safe is HeyReach for your LinkedIn accounts in 2026?

The safety question is the one that drives most HeyReach reviews in 2026, and the answer depends on the number of profiles and the structure of the outreach.

For a single LinkedIn account: HeyReach is safe at low to moderate volume, but the platform is not built for single-account use. The pricing is higher than alternatives like BriskReach BYO ($39) or Dripify ($59), and the per-profile safety controls are not meaningfully better than Expandi's dedicated IP architecture. For a single account at fewer than 200 connection requests per month, Expandi or BriskReach BYO is the safer pick.

For 5 to 10 accounts on a single dashboard: HeyReach's account rotation is theoretically safer than running 5 separate Octopus CRM profiles. The platform distributes volume across the connected accounts, which reduces per-account detection risk. The cluster-detection risk is real but manageable at this scale.

For 10+ accounts on a single dashboard: The cluster-detection risk scales with the number of profiles. LinkedIn's 2026 detection systems track IP relationships across accounts in the same workspace, device fingerprint patterns across logged-in sessions, and connection-graph similarity across prospect lists. When 10+ accounts on the same HeyReach dashboard all send connection requests to overlapping prospect lists within the same time window, LinkedIn flags the cluster. Agencies report losing multiple client accounts simultaneously after a single detection event.

The structural problem: HeyReach runs automation through LinkedIn accounts. The platform's safety controls — rotation, warmup, jitter, timezone targeting — reduce the probability of detection, but they do not eliminate the target. LinkedIn's 2026 enforcement is built to identify automation behavior, and no amount of configuration removes the risk of running automation through accounts that matter.

For teams that cannot afford to lose a primary LinkedIn account under any scenario, the categorical alternative is BriskReach Reps — a real-human model that deploys contracted human Reps to run outreach from their own profiles, eliminating the automation target entirely.
