Octopus CRM Pricing in 2026: Plans, Hidden Costs, and Cheaper Alternatives

MR Marcus R. · outreachbench_ · Updated May 2026

The Octopus CRM pricing page shows three plans at $9.99, $39.99, and $74.99 per month. That is the sticker price. The real cost of running Octopus CRM at scale in 2026 is meaningfully higher, and the gap between sticker and real is what drives most of the Octopus CRM alternatives searches in this category.

This article breaks down what Octopus CRM actually costs, the hidden costs that show up after you start using it, and the cheaper alternatives that fit specific use cases. The short version: BriskReach BYO mode at $39 per seat is the cheapest cloud-based Octopus CRM alternative with the most conservative safety engine on the market, and BriskReach Reps at $129 per Rep per month is the only model in the category that eliminates the automation risk entirely.

What is Octopus CRM, and what does the pricing actually buy?

Octopus CRM is a Chrome extension launched in 2018, built around the core LinkedIn automation features — auto-connect, auto-message, auto-visit, auto-endorse, and auto-follow. The platform is one of the most popular LinkedIn automation tools in the category, with more than 100,000 Chrome Web Store downloads and a Trustpilot presence clustered around 3.8 out of 5.

The pricing is the cheapest in the category. Three plans, all priced per LinkedIn account, all with a 14-day free trial on annual billing.

What are the Octopus CRM pricing tiers in 2026?

Octopus CRM ships three pricing tiers in 2026, each tied to the number of LinkedIn accounts you connect and the campaign features you unlock. The tiers are designed to grow with solo operators, but the architecture — a Chrome extension — carries the same structural detection risk at every tier.

Solo plan — $9.99 per month (or $7.99 per month billed annually)

  • 1 LinkedIn account
  • 100 connection requests per day
  • Auto-connect, auto-message, auto-visit
  • 3 active campaigns
  • Basic analytics
  • Email support

The Solo plan is the cheapest functional LinkedIn automation tier in the category. At $9.99 per month, no cloud-based alternative matches the price. The trade-off is the architecture — Chrome extension-based automation is the most heavily detected category in LinkedIn's 2026 enforcement, and the restriction rate for accounts running the Solo plan at the 100-connection daily cap is higher than cloud-based equivalents.

Pro plan — $39.99 per month (or $31.99 per month billed annually)

  • 3 LinkedIn accounts
  • 200 connection requests per day
  • A/B testing on message variants
  • 10 active campaigns
  • Advanced analytics
  • Priority email support

The Pro plan is the most common pick for solo operators who want to run multiple LinkedIn accounts from a single dashboard. The Agency plan is the most expensive Octopus CRM tier and the most expensive entry point in this category. At $74.99 per month, the Agency plan is roughly 1.9x the price of BriskReach BYO at $39 per seat for a single user, and meaningfully more expensive than HeyReach's $199 unlimited plan when you consider that HeyReach covers 50+ accounts on the same tier. The trade-off is the per-account volume — 400 connection requests per day on 10 accounts is high-volume usage that triggers cluster detection in LinkedIn's 2026 enforcement.

What are the hidden costs of Octopus CRM pricing?

The sticker price is the first number. The real cost of running Octopus CRM at scale in 2026 includes four categories of hidden cost that the pricing page does not show.

Account restriction recovery. When a LinkedIn account gets restricted while running Octopus CRM, recovery requires either an appeal through LinkedIn's help center (which can take 2 to 6 weeks) or a complete account rebuild (which costs 30 to 60 days of pipeline). The cost of a single restriction event for a sales team running Octopus at $74.99 per month is roughly $5,000 to $15,000 in lost pipeline, depending on the account's role in the team's outreach. (For a deeper look at why restrictions happen, see LinkedIn Account Restricted by Automation: What's Actually Happening and Why HeyReach, Aimfox, and Expandi Keep Getting LinkedIn Accounts Banned in 2026.)

Multi-account proxy infrastructure. Octopus CRM does not include proxy rotation, which means each connected LinkedIn account on the Pro or Agency plan must be paired with a residential or mobile proxy to avoid fingerprint correlation. Premium residential proxies cost $10 to $30 per month per account. The Agency plan at 10 accounts means $100 to $300 per month in additional proxy costs that the $74.99 sticker does not cover.

Browser fingerprint randomization. Chrome extensions expose browser fingerprints that LinkedIn can detect, even when proxied. Users running Octopus CRM at scale typically pair the extension with an anti-detect browser like GoLogin or Multilogin, which adds another $30 to $100 per month per account. The Agency plan at 10 accounts means $300 to $1,000 per month in additional anti-detect browser costs.

Customer support response time. Octopus CRM's support response time on the Solo and Pro plans averages 24 to 48 hours, which is slow when your LinkedIn account is restricted and you need help immediately. Multiple 2026 reviews flag this as a meaningful operational risk. The Agency plan includes priority support, but the underlying restriction problem does not go away — the support just responds faster to tickets about restrictions that have already happened.

When you add proxy costs, anti-detect browser costs, and the pipeline cost of a single restriction event, the real monthly cost of running Octopus CRM's Agency plan at scale is roughly $1,200 to $2,500 per month, not the $74.99 shown on the pricing page. Cloud-based alternatives like BriskReach, Dripify, and HeyReach include proxy infrastructure in the sticker price, which is the structural reason their pricing is higher but their real cost is often lower.
